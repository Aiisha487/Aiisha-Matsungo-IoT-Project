\section{Component Justifications}

\subsection{MAX9814 microphone breakout -- \$7.95 (Adafruit \#1713)}
Electret microphone with integrated AGC amplifier. SNR 61\,dBA provides
12\,dBA margin above 45\,dBA detection threshold. Analog output compatible
with ESP8266 \texttt{analogRead()} -- 2-hour implementation vs 8+ hours for
I2S digital alternative. Selected over cheaper alternatives per PES v1
Section~4 Conflict~2: implementation simplicity prioritised over marginal
cost savings given 10-week timeline constraint.

\subsection{Hammond 1551 ABS enclosure -- \$4.50}
Downgraded from Hammond 1591 series (\$8.00 in PES v1) to 1551 series
(\$4.50) -- smaller footprint appropriate for wall-mounted microphone sensor,
saves \$7.00 for 2 nodes. Still professional appearance for institutional
deployment (libraries, healthcare facilities). IP54 rating protects against
dust and splashing adequate for indoor HVAC-controlled environment.

\subsection{USB power supply -- \$2.00 per node}
Wall-powered via standard 5V/1A USB adapters (phone chargers repurposed).
Per-node consumption: 89.1\,mA @ 5V = 445\,mW continuous (PES v1 Section~6).
Annual electricity cost \$0.59/node at \$0.15/kWh -- negligible.
Eliminates battery constraints and enables 100\% duty cycle.
